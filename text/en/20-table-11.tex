%%K t11-tit-1 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Outskirts.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t11-c1-01-1 p
1. --- I live in a large town a hundred kilometres from the ocean, which is nevertheless a
first-rate \VUgras{port} \textsuperscript{(1)}. The river that runs along it is four hundred
metres wide and forms a very fine roadstead.

%%K t11-c1-02-1 p
2. --- The whole part of the town along the \VUgras{quays} \textsuperscript{(2)} has an
entirely maritime and industrial look, and forms a \VUgras{suburb} \textsuperscript{(3)} lived
in only by sailors and workers. The latter work in the
\VUgras{factories} \textsuperscript{(4)} and at the \VUgras{gasworks} \textsuperscript{(5)},
whose tall \VUgras{chimney} \textsuperscript{(6)} and \VUgras{gasholder} can be seen from a
long way off.

%%K t11-c1-03-1 p
3. --- In the Middle Ages the town was fortified, and some remains of its ramparts can still
be found, in particular the \VUgras{gate} \textsuperscript{(8)} of the old
\VUgras{fortified castle} \textsuperscript{(7)} flanked by its two
\VUgras{towers} \textsuperscript{(9)}, which are now used as a \VUgras{prison}.

%%K t11-c1-04-1 p
4. --- In this whole \VUgras{quarter}, which looks very old, only one building is
comparatively modern: the \VUgras{customs house} \textsuperscript{(10)}. It stands between the
quay and the little \VUgras{street} \textsuperscript{(11)} leading to the old
\VUgras{town hall} \textsuperscript{(12)}. That last building is easy to recognise by the
giant \VUgras{belfry} \textsuperscript{(13)} that towers over the old city.

%%K t11-c1-05-1 p
5. --- On the other side of the street \VUgras{stands} a building in the same style as the
customs house: it is the \VUgras{exchange} \textsuperscript{(14)}. One of its fronts looks on
to a small square where the
\VUgras{prefecture} \textsuperscript{(15)} is. Our town is not a capital, but it is
still an important county town.

%%K t11-tit-2 sub
\VUsaut{5.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Upper Part of the Town.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t11-c1-06-1 p
6. --- The garrison, a fairly large one, is housed in vast and very healthy \VUgras{barracks}
\textsuperscript{(16)}; they stretch as far as the railings of the \VUgras{municipal park}
\textsuperscript{(17)}. The \VUgras{boulevard} \textsuperscript{(19)} leading to this park
passes behind the \VUgras{savings bank} \textsuperscript{(18)} and comes out on the
\VUgras{cathedral} \textsuperscript{(20)} square.

%%K t11-c1-07-1 p
7. --- All these buildings rise in tiers on a small hill where our large
\VUgras{secondary school} \textsuperscript{(21)} also stands.

%%K t11-c1-07-2 p
If you go down a slightly sloping road that joins the High Street, you come to the
\VUgras{law courts} \textsuperscript{(22)}, in front of which lies a pleasant
\VUgras{public garden} \textsuperscript{(26)}. This \VUgras{square} is not very large, but in
the middle of the town it makes an oasis of greenery and cool air. So the townspeople use it a
lot, and like to come and sit under the awning of the \VUgras{café} \textsuperscript{(25)} to
listen to the military band that plays on Thursdays and Sundays in the
\VUgras{bandstand} \textsuperscript{(27)} set among the lawns and flower beds.

%%K t11-c1-08-1 p
8. --- On one of those lawns stands a bronze \VUgras{statue} \textsuperscript{(28)}, a
monument raised to the memory of one of our townsmen who did great service to the town where
he was born.

%%K t11-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Getting about.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t11-c1-09-1 p
9. --- Our town is not yet lit by electric light; it has only
\VUgras{gas lamps} \textsuperscript{(29)}. But the \VUgras{local press} is campaigning
for that system of lighting to be improved, \VUgras{just as} the tramways'
\VUgras{system of traction} has already been improved. \VUgras{The} old
carriages \VUgras{drawn} by \VUgras{horses} have been replaced by practical
\VUgras{electric trams} \textsuperscript{(31)}, which carry passengers quickly from one
end of \VUgras{the town to the other} at a very \VUgras{low} fare.

%%K t11-c1-10-1 p
10. --- Near the law courts is the \VUgras{bank} \textsuperscript{(32)}, where commercial
bills are discounted. The \VUgras{bank messengers} \textsuperscript{(33)} go round to people's
homes to collect what is owed.

%%K t11-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Art and Learning.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t11-c1-11-1 p
11. --- The fine building close by is the \VUgras{museum}. It houses at once the town
\VUgras{library} \textsuperscript{(34)}, some fine
\VUgras{natural history collections} \textsuperscript{(35)} (a natural history
museum) and a graceful \VUgras{picture gallery} \textsuperscript{(36)} that makes a splendid
permanent \VUgras{exhibition}.

%%K t11-c1-11-2 p
It is open to the public twice a week, but visitors can see it any day for a small tip to the
\VUgras{attendant} \textsuperscript{(37)}. \VUgras{Painters} \textsuperscript{(38)} come to
work there. You can see them \VUgras{in front of} their easels, \VUgras{palette} in hand,
copying the famous pictures.

%%K t11-c1-12-1 p
12. --- On the heights behind the museum you can just make out the
\VUgras{dome} \textsuperscript{(40)} of the \VUgras{hospital} \textsuperscript{(39)} and the
buildings of the \VUgras{teachers' training college} \textsuperscript{(41)} where the teachers
of our state schools were trained. On Theatre Square there is a
\VUgras{post office} \textsuperscript{(43)} housed in a \VUgras{private house}
\textsuperscript{(42)}.

%%K t11-tit-5 sub
\VUsaut{5.0mm}
\VUpk{11.4pt}{40}{The Fire.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t11-tit-6 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Cry of Fire.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t11-c2-01-1 p
1. --- Alarming news is spreading through the town: fire has just broken out at the theatre!
By the time I reach the \VUgras{square} \textsuperscript{(96)}, the crowd has already gathered
on the \VUgras{pavement} \textsuperscript{(46)} and on the
\VUgras{roadway} \textsuperscript{(47)}. The \VUgras{postal clerks} \textsuperscript{(44)} and the
\VUgras{postmen} \textsuperscript{(45)} are coming out of the post office. A
\VUgras{tram driver} \textsuperscript{(48)} has had to stop his tram to let through a
municipal \VUgras{ambulance} \textsuperscript{(50)} arriving with a \VUgras{surgeon}
\textsuperscript{(52)} and a \VUgras{nurse} \textsuperscript{(51)} to attend to an
\VUgras{injured man} \textsuperscript{(53)} carried on a \VUgras{stretcher}
\textsuperscript{(54)} to a spot near the \VUgras{newspaper kiosk} \textsuperscript{(55)}.

%%K t11-c2-02-1 p
2. --- A company of infantry on its way back from drill has stopped to help keep order with
the mounted \VUgras{municipal guards} \textsuperscript{(61)}. The
\VUgras{horsemen}, whose horses rear up, do better than the \VUgras{soldiers}
\textsuperscript{(57)} or the \VUgras{gendarmes} \textsuperscript{(63)} at pushing back the
\VUgras{onlookers} \textsuperscript{(56)}. The gendarmes have their hands full anyway. One of
them is telling the \VUgras{police officers} \textsuperscript{(64)} where to carry another
\VUgras{casualty} \textsuperscript{(65)}, a brave fireman who has fallen from a ladder.

%%K t11-c2-03-1 p
3. --- The \VUgras{officer} \textsuperscript{(66)} points out exactly where the
\VUgras{steam pump} \textsuperscript{(67)} now arriving must be placed. While waiting
for that powerful machine, he has had a \VUgras{hand pump} \textsuperscript{(68)} set up,
whose long \VUgras{hose} \textsuperscript{(69)} pours torrents of water on the fire. Six
firemen are working it, while their comrade, holding the
\VUgras{nozzle} \textsuperscript{(70)}, aims the \VUgras{jet} \textsuperscript{(71)} at the heart
of the blaze.

%%K t11-c2-04-1 p
4. --- On the other side of the theatre the big \VUgras{ladder} \textsuperscript{(72)} has
been raised. It lets the firemen get close to the flames leaping up amid swirls of black
\VUgras{smoke} \textsuperscript{(75)}.

%%K t11-tit-7 sub
\VUsaut{5.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Brave Deeds.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t11-c2-05-1 p
5. --- The \VUgras{fire} \textsuperscript{(74)} started in the foyer of the
\VUgras{theatre} \textsuperscript{(73)}, during a rehearsal of the \VUgras{opera} whose
first \VUgras{performance} was due tomorrow. Fortunately there were only a few
\VUgras{spectators} \textsuperscript{(76)} in the auditorium. The poor people took refuge on
the \VUgras{balcony} \textsuperscript{(77)}, where brave
\VUgras{firemen} \textsuperscript{(86)} are coming to their help with a ladder and
ropes.

%%K t11-c2-06-1 p
6. --- Under the \VUgras{portico} \textsuperscript{(78)}, where the \VUgras{playbill}
\textsuperscript{(79)} announces the performance, the \VUgras{musicians}
\textsuperscript{(81)} of the \VUgras{orchestra} \textsuperscript{(80)} can be seen fleeing,
carrying off their instruments: \VUgras{violin} \textsuperscript{(81)}, \VUgras{flute}
\textsuperscript{(82)}, \VUgras{cello} \textsuperscript{(83)}, \VUgras{trombone}
\textsuperscript{(84)},
\VUgras{drum} \textsuperscript{(85)}, and so on. People are trying to save part
of the \VUgras{furniture} \textsuperscript{(87)}.

%%K t11-c2-07-1 p
7. --- The \VUgras{actors} \textsuperscript{(89)} and \VUgras{actresses}
\textsuperscript{(88)},
\VUgras{singers} both men and women, have had to run out in their stage
\VUgras{costumes} \textsuperscript{(90)}. The tenor is explaining to a
\VUgras{journalist} \textsuperscript{(92)} how it happened. \VUgras{The reporter} came
running from the very first moment along with the authorities: the
\VUgras{prefect} \textsuperscript{(91)}, the \VUgras{mayor} \textsuperscript{(93)}, the
\VUgras{superintendent of police} \textsuperscript{(94)} and a \VUgras{magistrate}
\textsuperscript{(95)}, who will hold an inquiry to establish where the responsibility lies.
