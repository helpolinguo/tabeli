%%K t16-01-1 p
1. --- The new year is coming. The big shops have got ready their displays of \VUgras{toys}
\textsuperscript{(1)} and new year presents.

%%K t16-01-2 p
I asked my grandfather to take me to the bazaar to see this fine display, and he agreed.

%%K t16-02-1 p
2. --- First we stopped in front of some handsome trophies of arms holding a \VUgras{rifle}, a
\VUgras{peaked cap}, a \VUgras{sabre}, a \VUgras{sword belt} and a pair of
\VUgras{epaulettes}, or else a \VUgras{helmet}, a
\VUgras{breastplate} \textsuperscript{(3)} and a \VUgras{pistol} \textsuperscript{(4)}. All that
interested me far more than the \VUgras{magic lanterns} \textsuperscript{(2)}.

%%K t16-tit-1 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Fine sights.}
\VUsaut{2.6mm}

%%K t16-03-1 p
3. --- In the same department there was a \VUgras{sentry} \textsuperscript{(5)} in his
\VUgras{sentry box}; he seemed to be standing guard over a whole cardboard
menagerie. What a lot of animals there were! A \VUgras{parrot} \textsuperscript{(6)} on its
perch, a \VUgras{rhinoceros} \textsuperscript{(7)} with a horn on its nose, a
\VUgras{reindeer} \textsuperscript{(8)}, an \VUgras{ostrich} \textsuperscript{(9)}, a \VUgras{monkey}
\textsuperscript{(10)} cracking a nut, a \VUgras{fox} \textsuperscript{(11)} carrying off a
hen, a
\VUgras{crocodile} \textsuperscript{(12)} gaping with its enormous jaws, a
\VUgras{camel} \textsuperscript{(13)}, an elegant \VUgras{greyhound} \textsuperscript{(14)}, a
\VUgras{panther} \textsuperscript{(15)}, and then two beasts I did not know, which are, I
am told, a \VUgras{weasel} \textsuperscript{(16)} and a \VUgras{hyena} \textsuperscript{(17)}.
There was also a \VUgras{leopard} \textsuperscript{(18)} and a \VUgras{wolf}
\textsuperscript{(21)}; close by were sweet little \VUgras{rats} \textsuperscript{(19)} and
tiny rubber \VUgras{mice} \textsuperscript{(20)}. Lastly a \VUgras{hippopotamus}
\textsuperscript{(22)} and a humped
\VUgras{dromedary} \textsuperscript{(23)} completed the collection. I would have stayed
there for hours if a splendid camp full of
\VUgras{tin soldiers} \textsuperscript{(29)} nearby had not caught my eye.

%%K t16-tit-2 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Soldiers and war.}
\VUsaut{2.8mm}

%%K t16-04-1 p
4. --- My grandfather, who is a \VUgras{disabled veteran} \textsuperscript{(24)} and had to
leave the army after a \VUgras{wound} that earned him the
\VUgras{military medal}, loves to tell me about the \VUgras{campaigns} he
took part in. He was delighted to explain to me the various movements of the little army in
miniature. A group of real soldiers visiting the bazaar --- an \VUgras{engineer}
\textsuperscript{(25)}, an \VUgras{alpine light infantryman} \textsuperscript{(26)} in his
\VUgras{beret}, an infantry \VUgras{sergeant major} \textsuperscript{(27)} and an artillery
\VUgras{sergeant} \textsuperscript{(28)} with a gold
\VUgras{stripe} on his sleeve --- stopped near us to listen to the
explanations.

%%K t16-05-1 p
5. --- My grandfather, very proud to have such a distinguished audience, improvised a whole
plan of battle.

%%K t16-05-2 p
The fortified town, with its \VUgras{ramparts} and \VUgras{ditches}, was besieged by the
\VUgras{enemy army}, which, after a long
\VUgras{bombardment}, was preparing to \VUgras{storm} it. But the
\VUgras{besieged}, driven to it by hunger, had attempted a \VUgras{sortie}
against the \VUgras{camp} of the \VUgras{besiegers}. Having beaten off the
\VUgras{attack} in a fierce \VUgras{fight} around the \VUgras{tents}, the
\VUgras{victorious} besiegers sent their \VUgras{squadrons} in
\VUgras{pursuit} of the \VUgras{defeated} attackers. The latter
\VUgras{retreated}, leaving the \VUgras{battlefield} strewn with the
\VUgras{dead} and the \VUgras{wounded}. \VUgras{Stretcher bearers} carried the
wounded off to the \VUgras{field hospital} to be tended by the
\VUgras{surgeon}.

%%K t16-05-3 p
Despite the heroic resistance of a few \VUgras{battalions} that had formed
\VUgras{square}, the \VUgras{defeat} was complete; the rest of the army
\VUgras{fled}, leaving many \VUgras{prisoners} behind. The enemy was about to
crown its \VUgras{victory} by taking the town. Perhaps that would be the end of the campaign,
and after an \VUgras{armistice} a \VUgras{peace treaty} could be signed.

%%K t16-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{4.4mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{New year presents.}
\VUsaut{2.8mm}

%%K t16-06-1 p
6. --- The young soldiers, who laughed as they listened to the veteran's colourful tale, asked
him a few more questions. As for me, I walked round the counter to look at a fine
\VUgras{crossbow} \textsuperscript{(32)} and a
\VUgras{bow} \textsuperscript{(33)} with its \VUgras{arrow} \textsuperscript{(31)}. There I found
another collection of animals: two \VUgras{songbirds} \textsuperscript{(34)}, a clockwork
\VUgras{nightingale} and \VUgras{warbler} singing at the top of their voices, and a series of
strange beasts --- a \VUgras{bat} \textsuperscript{(35)}, a \VUgras{boa}
\textsuperscript{(36)}, a \VUgras{tortoise} \textsuperscript{(37)}, a
\VUgras{seal} \textsuperscript{(38)}, a \VUgras{whale} \textsuperscript{(39)} and a \VUgras{shark}
\textsuperscript{(40)} made of goldbeater's skin.

%%K t16-07-1 p
7. --- I did not stop long to look at them, because I had caught sight of the thing I had been
dreaming of: a magnificent \VUgras{puppet theatre} \textsuperscript{(41)} with splendid
\VUgras{scenery} and a delightful red \VUgras{curtain}. On
\VUgras{stage}, two actors seemed to be playing a \VUgras{part} in a most
interesting \VUgras{play}, because the little cardboard \VUgras{spectators} in the boxes, or
seated in the \VUgras{stalls} and in the \VUgras{pit} behind the
\VUgras{conductor}, were applauding warmly.

%%K t16-07-2 p
Sadly, that theatre was very expensive.

%%K t16-08-1 p
8. --- Choosing among the toys was difficult anyway, because the windows were piled with toys
of every kind: \VUgras{Harlequin} \textsuperscript{(42)},
\VUgras{Punch} \textsuperscript{(43)}, \VUgras{Pierrot} \textsuperscript{(44)}, \VUgras{owls}
\textsuperscript{(45)} flapping their wings, whistling \VUgras{blackbirds}
\textsuperscript{(46)}, barking \VUgras{poodles}, a \VUgras{gramophone} \textsuperscript{(47)}
and
\VUgras{puzzles}. I loved one of these toys: it showed a
\VUgras{sea battle} \textsuperscript{(48)} in which a \VUgras{ship} is attacked by
\VUgras{pirates} off a shore planted with \VUgras{coconut palms}.

9. --- I could look at all these wonders quite at my leisure, because there were only a few
people in the store --- barely a handful of idlers, a
\VUgras{dragoon} \textsuperscript{(49)}, and a \VUgras{collector} \textsuperscript{(50)} presenting
a \VUgras{bill of exchange} at the main \VUgras{cash desk} \textsuperscript{(51)}.

%%K t16-10-1 p
10. --- I asked my grandfather what the books on the shelves behind the
\VUgras{cashier} were for; he told me they were the \VUgras{account books}.

%%K t16-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{3.6mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Gazing round the shop.}
\VUsaut{2.8mm}

%%K t16-11-1 p
11. --- Leaving the toy department, we went to the saddlery to glance at the \VUgras{saddles}
\textsuperscript{(53)}, \VUgras{stirrups} \textsuperscript{(54)},
\VUgras{bridles} \textsuperscript{(55)}, \VUgras{bits} \textsuperscript{(56)} and
\VUgras{riding crops}. While the \VUgras{department manager} \textsuperscript{(57)} gave

\VUgras{china} and \VUgras{glassware} \textsuperscript{(58)}, where there were fine
\VUgras{salad bowls} and delicate \VUgras{teapots} \textsuperscript{(59)}.

%%K t16-12-1 p
12. --- To go up to the first floor, we passed behind the window of the
\VUgras{corsets} \textsuperscript{(60)}, next to the hosiery, where very fine
\VUgras{drawers} \textsuperscript{(63)} were laid out. Nearby, a \VUgras{shop assistant}
\textsuperscript{(61)} was helping a \VUgras{customer} \textsuperscript{(62)} choose fine silk
\VUgras{fabrics} \textsuperscript{(64)}.

%%K t16-12-2 p
As I went up the stairs, I noticed that the store was decorated with Japanese
\VUgras{lanterns} \textsuperscript{(65)}, \VUgras{parasols} \textsuperscript{(66)} and
enormous \VUgras{palms} \textsuperscript{(70)}.

%%K t16-13-1 p
13. --- On the first floor there was not much to see: only furniture (*),
\VUgras{safes} \textsuperscript{(67)}, \VUgras{chests of drawers} \textsuperscript{(68)} and
\VUgras{prams} \textsuperscript{(69)}. But the general view of the store was very
\VUgras{entertaining}.

%%K t16-14-1 p

\VUgras{guitars} \textsuperscript{(72)}, \VUgras{mandolins} \textsuperscript{(73)},
\VUgras{cornets} \textsuperscript{(74)}, \VUgras{clarinets} \textsuperscript{(75)}, violins with

\textsuperscript{(80)}.

%%K t16-14-2 p
In the middle of the store stood a tall \VUgras{Christmas tree} \textsuperscript{(81)} hung
all over with candles, trinkets and toys of every kind:
\VUgras{wooden horses} \textsuperscript{(82)}, \VUgras{dolls} \textsuperscript{(83)}, and so on.

%%K t16-14-3 p
The \VUgras{perfumery} \textsuperscript{(84)}, with its \VUgras{tooth washes} and its various
\VUgras{perfumes}, gave off a very pleasant smell that rose to us.

stopped for a moment in front of an
\VUgras{astronomy chart} \textsuperscript{(85)} showing, he told me, \VUgras{comets}
\textsuperscript{(a)}, \VUgras{eclipses of the moon and the sun} \textsuperscript{(b)}, a

\VUgras{(north, south, east and west)}, a map of the two
\VUgras{hemispheres} \textsuperscript{(d)}, the \VUgras{planets} \textsuperscript{(e)} and the
\VUgras{solar system} \textsuperscript{(f)}. I understood none of it, and I was far

\VUgras{purses} \textsuperscript{(88)} and the \VUgras{wallets} \textsuperscript{(89)}.

%%K t16-16-1 p

\VUgras{belt buckles} \textsuperscript{(91)}, and the pretty
\VUgras{artificial flowers} \textsuperscript{(94)} gracefully arranged in baskets. The
\VUgras{violets}, \VUgras{wallflowers}, \VUgras{hyacinths} \textsuperscript{(95)},
\VUgras{geraniums} \textsuperscript{(96)}, \VUgras{lilies} \textsuperscript{(97)} and

\textsuperscript{(92)} in a box beside the \VUgras{hairpins} \textsuperscript{(93)}. He

%%K t16-18-1 p

\VUgras{bronzes} \textsuperscript{(101)}. A \VUgras{thief} \textsuperscript{(102)} had just
