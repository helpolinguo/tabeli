%%K t15-tit-1 sub
\VUpk{11.4pt}{40}{The Market. --- Food.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-01-1 p
1. --- Most of the traders who supply us with the food we need are
gathered under the \VUgras{hall} \textsuperscript{(1)} of the \VUgras{covered market}, or
in the neighbouring streets.

%%K t15-02-1 p
2. --- The \VUgras{pork butcher} \textsuperscript{(2)}, who sells \VUgras{ham}
\textsuperscript{(3)}, \VUgras{black pudding} \textsuperscript{(4)}, \VUgras{sausage}
\textsuperscript{(5)}, \VUgras{chitterling sausage} \textsuperscript{(6)} and \VUgras{bacon}
\textsuperscript{(7)}, stands next to the \VUgras{butter and cheese seller}
\textsuperscript{(8)}, whose stall is laid out with red-rinded
\VUgras{Dutch cheeses} \textsuperscript{(9)}, \VUgras{Camemberts} \textsuperscript{(10)}, great
\VUgras{wheels of Gruyère} \textsuperscript{(11)} and fresh \VUgras{pats of butter}
\textsuperscript{(12)}.

%%K t15-03-1 p
3. --- In front of her, a \VUgras{vegetable seller} \textsuperscript{(13)} offers her
customers fine \VUgras{tomatoes} \textsuperscript{(14)}, \VUgras{cauliflowers}
\textsuperscript{(15)}, \VUgras{lettuce} \textsuperscript{(16)}, \VUgras{cabbages} \textsuperscript{(17)},
\VUgras{pumpkins} \textsuperscript{(19)}, \VUgras{melons} \textsuperscript{(18)} and even
\VUgras{mushrooms} \textsuperscript{(20)}. But a \VUgras{brewer's man}, who has stopped
his \VUgras{delivery cart} \textsuperscript{(21)} loaded with \VUgras{beer barrels}
\textsuperscript{(22)} right in front of her stall, is keeping her customers from
getting near. A market gardener is bringing her a basket of
\VUgras{artichokes} \textsuperscript{(23)}.

%%K t15-tit-2 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Selling and Buying.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-04-1 p
4. --- In the market the bustle is great, for the hour of the
\VUgras{auction} \textsuperscript{(24)} has come. The \VUgras{housewives} \textsuperscript{(26)} who
come here to do their shopping stop on their way in front of the stall of
the \VUgras{tripe seller} \textsuperscript{(25)} to buy \VUgras{tripe} or a
\VUgras{calf's head}.

%%K t15-05-1 p
5. --- A stout \VUgras{cook} \textsuperscript{(27)} is haggling over a very fine
\VUgras{tuna} with the \VUgras{fishmonger} \textsuperscript{(28)}, who puts her prices up
as she pleases. She has had a big delivery of \VUgras{eels, sole, trout} and
\VUgras{carp}. Her \VUgras{cod} and her \VUgras{mussels} are very fresh.

%%K t15-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Cheap goods and dear.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-06-1 p
6. --- The \VUgras{poultry seller} \textsuperscript{(29)}, set up beside her, is very
honest. When she sells a \VUgras{turkey}, a \VUgras{goose}, a \VUgras{duck} or
a plain \VUgras{chicken}, she asks no more than the fair price.

%%K t15-07-1 p
7. --- At the corner of the square, on the first floor, a
\VUgras{linen draper} \textsuperscript{(30)} has recently set up shop, where buyers are
always sure to find a very fine choice of \VUgras{tablecloths},
\VUgras{napkins, aprons} and \VUgras{tea towels}. On the same floor, a
\VUgras{cutler} \textsuperscript{(31)} has set up his workshop. He sharpens the
\VUgras{knives} of the market women and makes the gardeners
\VUgras{pruning hooks} out of the hardest \VUgras{steel}.

%%K t15-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Grocery.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-08-1 p
8. --- Below his workshop a large food shop has opened not long ago. It is
no longer the plain, modest \VUgras{grocer's shop} \textsuperscript{(32)} of former
days, which sold only everyday goods --- \VUgras{tea} \textsuperscript{(33)},
\VUgras{chocolate} \textsuperscript{(34)}, \VUgras{loaf sugar} \textsuperscript{(37)} and
\VUgras{soap} \textsuperscript{(38)}. Here everything is sold:
\VUgras{crisp biscuits}, \VUgras{preserves} \textsuperscript{(35)}, \VUgras{liqueurs}
\textsuperscript{(36)}, \VUgras{rum, brandy, kirsch, anisette} and \VUgras{curaçao}. Game
is to be had here just as easily --- \VUgras{hare} \textsuperscript{(39)},
\VUgras{thrushes} \textsuperscript{(40)}, \VUgras{partridges} \textsuperscript{(41)},
\VUgras{quail} \textsuperscript{(42)}, \VUgras{wild duck} \textsuperscript{(43)} or
\VUgras{pheasant} \textsuperscript{(44)} --- as tropical fruit such as \VUgras{bananas}
\textsuperscript{(46)} and \VUgras{pineapples}.

%%K t15-09-1 p
9. --- A very obliging grocer's \VUgras{assistant} \textsuperscript{(45)} is ready to
hand over whatever is asked for: \VUgras{jam} \textsuperscript{(47)}, redcurrant or
quince, \VUgras{dripping} \textsuperscript{(48)}, \VUgras{gherkins} \textsuperscript{(49)} or salted
\VUgras{sardines} \textsuperscript{(50)}.

%%K t15-09-2 p
That is very handy for the \VUgras{customers} \textsuperscript{(51)}, who find, next to
the most ordinary goods, the rarest early produce and the tastiest fruit:
juicy \VUgras{pears} \textsuperscript{(52)}, \VUgras{plums} \textsuperscript{(53)}, \VUgras{grapes}
\textsuperscript{(54)}, \VUgras{peaches} \textsuperscript{(55)}, \VUgras{almonds} \textsuperscript{(56)} and
\VUgras{walnuts} \textsuperscript{(57)}.

%%K t15-tit-5 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Customers.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-10-1 p
10. --- The \VUgras{rice} \textsuperscript{(58)} and the \VUgras{lentils} \textsuperscript{(59)} stand
beside the box of smoked \VUgras{herrings} \textsuperscript{(60)}; the \VUgras{potato}
\textsuperscript{(61)} and the \VUgras{bean} \textsuperscript{(63)} sit next to the
\VUgras{apples} \textsuperscript{(62)}, \VUgras{figs} \textsuperscript{(64)}, \VUgras{prunes}
\textsuperscript{(65)} and \VUgras{hazelnuts} \textsuperscript{(66)}. Fresh \VUgras{eggs}
\textsuperscript{(67)} and \VUgras{olives} \textsuperscript{(68)} are to be had in this shop as
well, so \VUgras{baskets} \textsuperscript{(70)} and \VUgras{string bags} \textsuperscript{(73)}
fill up very fast indeed.

%%K t15-11-1 p
11. --- The \VUgras{coffee} \textsuperscript{(72)} of this excellent firm has earned a
well-deserved name among the \VUgras{maids} \textsuperscript{(69)} and the cooks, for
the old \VUgras{grocer} \textsuperscript{(71)} roasts it himself with the greatest care.

%%K t15-tit-6 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Things to see.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-12-1 p
12. --- Not everyone comes to the market to buy provisions. In the
picturesque coming and going of the little street that leads to the hall,
the most varied people are to be seen. Beside a good-natured
\VUgras{slaughterhouse man} \textsuperscript{(75)} pushing a \VUgras{wheelbarrow}
\textsuperscript{(74)} in front of him, here are two soldiers on leave, very proud
to show off their bright uniforms: the \VUgras{hussar} \textsuperscript{(76)} in his
blue \VUgras{dolman} and \VUgras{plumed shako}, and the
\VUgras{corporal of Zouaves} in baggy breeches with a \VUgras{fez} on his
head.

%%K t15-13-1 p
13. --- The \VUgras{baker} \textsuperscript{(80)}, standing on the doorstep of the
\VUgras{bakery} \textsuperscript{(78)}, has just kneaded the dough for his \VUgras{bread}
\textsuperscript{(79)} and taken out of the oven the \VUgras{croissants} \textsuperscript{(81)},
the \VUgras{rolls} \textsuperscript{(82)} and the \VUgras{round loaves} \textsuperscript{(83)} that
are in the window.

%%K t15-14-1 p
14. --- Above the bakery lives a skilful \VUgras{seamstress} \textsuperscript{(84)} who
employs several \VUgras{embroiderers} \textsuperscript{(85)}. Next to her lives a
\VUgras{laundress and ironer} \textsuperscript{(86)}, who starches the \VUgras{linen}
\textsuperscript{(88)} after washing and drying it, and presses it with an
\VUgras{iron} \textsuperscript{(87)}.

%%K t15-tit-7 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Meat, fish and vegetables.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-15-1 p
15. --- In the \VUgras{butcher's shop} \textsuperscript{(89)} on the ground floor, meat
of every kind is sold --- \VUgras{mutton} \textsuperscript{(90)}, \VUgras{beef}
\textsuperscript{(94)} or \VUgras{veal} \textsuperscript{(92)} --- as \VUgras{legs of lamb,
steaks, joints} and \VUgras{chops}. The \VUgras{butcher's boy} \textsuperscript{(93)}
cuts up all this meat and sets aside the \VUgras{marrowbones} \textsuperscript{(96)}. At
the door of the butcher's stands an \VUgras{oyster seller} \textsuperscript{(97)} who
sells \VUgras{oysters} \textsuperscript{(98)}. She opens them if you wish.

%%K t15-16-1 p
16. --- All these traders pay a licence or a pitch fee; that is why the
\VUgras{policeman} \textsuperscript{(100)} pursues without mercy the poor
\VUgras{street vegetable sellers} \textsuperscript{(99)}, who compete with them by
carrying about on their barrows \VUgras{carrots, radishes, turnips, leeks}
or \VUgras{bundles of asparagus, fresh peas, spinach, sorrel, watercress}
and \VUgras{parsley}.

%%K t15-tit-8 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Look out for the policeman!}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t15-17-1 p
17. --- The boy selling \VUgras{garlic} \textsuperscript{(101)} and \VUgras{onions} knows
very well that he too is not allowed to sell his goods near the market.
He runs off, at the risk of upsetting the basket of \VUgras{crabs},
\VUgras{crayfish} and \VUgras{shrimps} belonging to the \VUgras{seller}
\textsuperscript{(102)} of \VUgras{spiny lobsters} and \VUgras{lobsters}.

%%K t15-18-1 p
18. --- Everyone is bustling about and making a great din; but that does
not stop one hearing clearly the call of the travelling \VUgras{glazier}
\textsuperscript{(103)}, nor the cry of the \VUgras{coalman} \textsuperscript{(104)}, who has just
left his cart for a moment to deliver a \VUgras{sack of coal} \textsuperscript{(105)}.
