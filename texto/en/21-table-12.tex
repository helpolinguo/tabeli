\VUtitre{15.0pt}{60}{CHART No. 12}
\VUsaut{2.4mm}
\VUpk{11.4pt}{40}{The Station. --- The Railways. --- The Boats.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}
\textsc{Note.} --- \textit{The timetables used in each country differ, so we have taken the
times of the}
\VUgras{French chart} \textit{as our example.}

%%K t12-01-1 p
1. --- I have just made a journey through Germany. Before setting out, I had bought a
\VUgras{timetable} \textsuperscript{(15)} to find out the departure times of the trains.
Having found that the most convenient train left Paris at nine in the evening and reached
Strasbourg at one thirteen, I made ready for my journey, and the next day I had myself driven
to the station.

%%K t12-02-1 p
2. --- When I came into the great departure hall, behind an old country
\VUgras{priest} \textsuperscript{(2)} carrying his \VUgras{travelling bag}
\textsuperscript{(3)}, a good many \VUgras{travellers} \textsuperscript{(1)} had already
arrived.

%%K t12-02-2 p
As I wanted to send a \VUgras{telegram} \textsuperscript{(5)}, I had an
\VUgras{inspector} \textsuperscript{(6)} point out the \VUgras{telegraph office}
\textsuperscript{(4)} to me.

%%K t12-03-1 p
3. --- Before going through to the \VUgras{waiting room} \textsuperscript{(7)}, I went to get
myself a return \VUgras{ticket} \textsuperscript{(9)}.

%%K t12-04-1 p
4. --- I did not wait long at the \VUgras{ticket window} \textsuperscript{(8)}, because there
were hardly any people there. A \VUgras{soldier} \textsuperscript{(10)} going off on leave
kindly gave me his turn, so I had time to ask a few questions of the \VUgras{stationmaster}
\textsuperscript{(13)}, who was talking with the \VUgras{police superintendent}
\textsuperscript{(12)} and the \VUgras{gendarme} \textsuperscript{(11)} on duty.

%%K t12-tit-1 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Registering the luggage.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t12-05-1 p
5. --- After buying a paper at the \VUgras{bookstall} \textsuperscript{(14)}, I had my
\VUgras{luggage} \textsuperscript{(17)} weighed --- a \VUgras{porter} \textsuperscript{(16)} had
just brought it up on a \VUgras{luggage trolley} \textsuperscript{(19)}. The
\VUgras{clerk} \textsuperscript{(22)} in charge of the \VUgras{weighing machine}
\textsuperscript{(21)} told me that my trunk weighed thirty-five kilograms, which made an
\VUgras{excess} of five kilograms, for which I had to pay a supplement at the
\VUgras{luggage window} \textsuperscript{(23)}.

%%K t12-06-1 p
6. --- As I was early, I had time to watch the movement of travellers about the station. Some
were leaving or collecting small bags at the
\VUgras{left luggage office} \textsuperscript{(25)}; others were consulting the
\VUgras{timetables} \textsuperscript{(26)}. Arriving travellers hurried towards the
\VUgras{exit} \textsuperscript{(32)} with their \VUgras{suitcases} \textsuperscript{(24)} in
hand. A few waited at the \VUgras{luggage delivery} \textsuperscript{(27)} and handed over
their \VUgras{ticket} \textsuperscript{(29)} to collect their trunks,
\VUgras{crates} \textsuperscript{(28)} or \VUgras{baskets} \textsuperscript{(30)}.

%%K t12-07-1 p
7. --- The \VUgras{excise officers} \textsuperscript{(33)} went carefully through the parcels
to see whether there was anything to declare.

%%K t12-08-1 p
8. --- Some travellers made for the \VUgras{refreshment room} \textsuperscript{(34)}, where a
\VUgras{waiter} \textsuperscript{(35)} was busy serving them. They had to hurry, because the
\VUgras{train} \textsuperscript{(36)}, an express, would not stay long in the station.

%%K t12-09-1 p
9. --- Then I went out on to the departure platform and looked for a through \VUgras{carriage}
\textsuperscript{(37)} so as not to have to change during the journey. I got into a corridor
coach with a \VUgras{compartment} \textsuperscript{(38)} for smokers and one reserved for
“ladies travelling alone”.

%%K t12-tit-2 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Setting off by night.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t12-10-1 p
10. --- After climbing the \VUgras{step} \textsuperscript{(40)}, shutting the
\VUgras{door} \textsuperscript{(39)}, rolling up the \VUgras{blind} \textsuperscript{(41)} and
putting my small bags in the \VUgras{luggage rack} \textsuperscript{(43)}, I sat down on the
\VUgras{seat} \textsuperscript{(42)}. Seeing the \VUgras{lamp man} \textsuperscript{(44)}
light the lamps, I remembered that I had a night to spend in the carriage and called the
attendant in charge of hiring out \VUgras{pillows} \textsuperscript{(45)} to ask him for one.

%%K t12-11-1 p
11. --- The guard came to check the tickets. I asked him why we had not left, since it was
already time. He answered that the
\VUgras{train chief} \textsuperscript{(46)} was busy having bicycles put in the
\VUgras{luggage van} \textsuperscript{(47)}. Besides, not all the \VUgras{foot warmers}
\textsuperscript{(48)} had been changed yet.

%%K t12-12-1 p
12. --- But we were about to leave: the \VUgras{fireman} \textsuperscript{(50)}, up on his
\VUgras{tender} \textsuperscript{(49)}, was taking coal to stoke the fire of the
\VUgras{locomotive} \textsuperscript{(54)}, and \VUgras{the driver} \textsuperscript{(51)} was
waiting only for the signal from the \VUgras{assistant stationmaster} \textsuperscript{(52)},
who stood on the \VUgras{platform} \textsuperscript{(53)}.

%%K t12-13-1 p
13. --- The \VUgras{boiler} \textsuperscript{(56)} of the locomotive rumbled dully; the
\VUgras{funnel} \textsuperscript{(55)} threw out torrents of smoke mixed with
\VUgras{steam} \textsuperscript{(60)}. A shrill \VUgras{whistle} \textsuperscript{(59)} rang out
and the \VUgras{pistons} \textsuperscript{(58)} began to move, driving the
\VUgras{wheels} \textsuperscript{(57)} of the heavy machine.

%%K t12-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Off we go!}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t12-14-1 p
14. --- The train, running on the \VUgras{rails} \textsuperscript{(64)}, picked up speed; the
\VUgras{platforms} \textsuperscript{(65)} came to an end; we passed the
\VUgras{lavatories} \textsuperscript{(66)}, and also a line of carriages standing on
another \VUgras{track} \textsuperscript{(63)}: \VUgras{sleeping cars} \textsuperscript{(67)},
a
\VUgras{restaurant car} \textsuperscript{(68)}, a \VUgras{mail van} \textsuperscript{(69)}.

%%K t12-noto-1 noto
\VUnotes{143.2mm}{%
\textit{(*) Note.} --- \textit{The journey is of course described as it was along the pre-war
frontier.}%
}

%%K t12-15-1 p
15. --- Then the \VUgras{goods station} \textsuperscript{(71)} passed before our eyes. Under
the sheds, clerks were loading the \VUgras{goods} \textsuperscript{(72)} sent by slow train.
\VUgras{Shunters} \textsuperscript{(76)} near the \VUgras{water tower} \textsuperscript{(73)}
were moving \VUgras{cattle trucks} \textsuperscript{(75)} and
\VUgras{flat wagons} \textsuperscript{(79)} to make up a \VUgras{goods train}
\textsuperscript{(80)}.

%%K t12-16-1 p
16. --- We crossed the last \VUgras{points} \textsuperscript{(78)}, beside which the signalman
was standing; we passed the \VUgras{signal} \textsuperscript{(86)} and the station vanished
into the distance.

%%K t12-17-1 p
17. --- Soon we went out on to the \VUgras{iron bridge} \textsuperscript{(74)} that crosses
the \VUgras{river} \textsuperscript{(81)}. Once over the bridge, we ran along the river, on
which powerful \VUgras{tugs} \textsuperscript{(82)} were pulling heavy
\VUgras{barges} \textsuperscript{(83)}. We also saw a \VUgras{passenger boat}
\textsuperscript{(84)} just coming alongside a \VUgras{landing stage} \textsuperscript{(85)}.

%%K t12-18-1 p
18. --- After racing through several stations, we went through a few
\VUgras{tunnels} \textsuperscript{(87)} and left behind us countless
\VUgras{cottages} \textsuperscript{(88)} belonging to the \VUgras{crossing keepers}.
Rocked by the swaying of the train, I fell into a deep sleep.

%%K t12-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Deutsch-Avricourt Station.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t12-19-1 p
19. --- I was suddenly woken by the voice of an official shouting: “Deutsch-Avricourt!
\textsuperscript{(*)} All change!” It was the customs inspection and all the tiresome
formalities of the frontier. I had to set my watch by the station \VUgras{clock}
\textsuperscript{(89)}, which was fifty-five minutes fast; barely awake, I could hardly tell
the \VUgras{big hand} \textsuperscript{(90)} from the \VUgras{little one}
\textsuperscript{(91)}; the \VUgras{face} \textsuperscript{(92)} itself was quite blurred by
the morning mist.

%%K t12-20-1 p
20. --- While the customs officers went through their inspection, I yawned my way through the
\VUgras{posters} \textsuperscript{(93)} that decorate the station walls. I had breakfast to
pass the time, consulted the map of the German \VUgras{network} \textsuperscript{(94)} to see
how far I still was from Strasbourg, and got back into my carriage, heartened by the hope of
soon reaching my journey's end.
