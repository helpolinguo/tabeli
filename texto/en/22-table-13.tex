%%K t13-01-1 p
1. --- I got to Royan yesterday evening and checked in at the \textit{Ocean Hotel}, which a
friend had recommended.

%%K t13-01-2 p
I was given a fine \VUgras{room} \textsuperscript{(2)} on the second \VUgras{floor}
\textsuperscript{(1)}, where I slept very well (*).

%%K t13-02-1 p
2. --- This morning, on leaving my room, where the \VUgras{maid} \textsuperscript{(3)} had
brought me my breakfast, I went into the
\VUgras{smoking room} \textsuperscript{(5)}, which also serves as a
\VUgras{reading room} \textsuperscript{(4)}, to look through the \VUgras{newspapers}
\textsuperscript{(6)} while smoking a \VUgras{cigarette} \textsuperscript{(8)}. When I had
finished reading, I went down in the \VUgras{lift} \textsuperscript{(9)} and set off for a
walk through the town.

%%K t13-03-1 p
3. --- As I had forgotten to ask the hotel \VUgras{clerk} \textsuperscript{(12)} at what time
meals were served at the \VUgras{table d'hôte} \textsuperscript{(11)}, I came back early and
took the chance to have a bath in the hotel
\VUgras{bathroom} \textsuperscript{(10)}. Then I went back to the \VUgras{cash desk}
\textsuperscript{(15)} to telephone one of my friends. But the \VUgras{telephone}
\textsuperscript{(13)} was engaged; seeing that I was at a loss, the
\VUgras{cashier} \textsuperscript{(14)}, who was entering a \VUgras{bill} \textsuperscript{(17)} in
the \VUgras{guest register} \textsuperscript{(16)}, asked the \VUgras{hall porter}
\textsuperscript{(18)} to send the \VUgras{page boy} \textsuperscript{(19)} to run my errand
on his \VUgras{bicycle} \textsuperscript{(20)}.

%%K t13-04-1 p
4. --- I thanked her and went out to tip the \VUgras{porter} \textsuperscript{(24)} (the
odd-job man), who had brought from the station on his
\VUgras{handcart} \textsuperscript{(25)} my \VUgras{hatbox} \textsuperscript{(26)}, my
\VUgras{suitcase} \textsuperscript{(27)} and my \VUgras{travelling bag} \textsuperscript{(28)}.
The \VUgras{postman} \textsuperscript{(21)}, arriving just then, handed me a
\VUgras{letter} \textsuperscript{(22)}.

%%K t13-05-1 p
5. --- I stayed a little longer on the doorstep, near the \VUgras{letterbox}
\textsuperscript{(23)}, watching the traffic in the street. The \VUgras{driver}
\textsuperscript{(30)} of the \VUgras{omnibus} \textsuperscript{(29)} was loading on to his
vehicle the \VUgras{trunk} \textsuperscript{(31)} of a \VUgras{guest} \textsuperscript{(32)}
to whom the \VUgras{hotel keeper} \textsuperscript{(33)} was saying goodbye. A young
\VUgras{telegraph boy} \textsuperscript{(35)} was bringing, in no hurry at all, a
\VUgras{telegram} \textsuperscript{(34)} for one of the hotel's guests; a
\VUgras{tourist} \textsuperscript{(36)}, with his \VUgras{camera} \textsuperscript{(38)} slung over
his shoulder, was consulting his \VUgras{guidebook} \textsuperscript{(37)}.

%%K t13-tit-1 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Lunchtime.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t13-06-1 p
6. --- In front of the railings, a placid \VUgras{errand man} \textsuperscript{(39)} was
dozing between his \VUgras{carrying hooks} \textsuperscript{(40)} and his
\VUgras{shoeshine box} \textsuperscript{(41)}, while a young \VUgras{laundress}
\textsuperscript{(42)} carrying a \VUgras{basket} \textsuperscript{(43)} of linen laughed at
the solemn air of the \VUgras{head waiter} \textsuperscript{(44)} standing by the door.

%%K t13-07-1 p
7. --- The sight of the \VUgras{cook} \textsuperscript{(45)}, who had come out of his
\VUgras{kitchen} \textsuperscript{(47)} in the \VUgras{basement} \textsuperscript{(48)} to send a
\VUgras{kitchen boy} \textsuperscript{(46)} on an errand, reminded me that lunchtime was
near, and I went to the restaurant across the yard, where a
\VUgras{motorist} \textsuperscript{(50)} was parking his \VUgras{car} \textsuperscript{(49)} while a
\VUgras{mechanic} \textsuperscript{(52)} was repairing, on instructions from the
\VUgras{cyclist} \textsuperscript{(53)}, a \VUgras{motor tricycle} \textsuperscript{(51)} that
had just had an accident.

%%K t13-08-1 p
8. --- I asked a young \VUgras{groom} \textsuperscript{(54)} carrying
\VUgras{glasses of beer} \textsuperscript{(55)} whether the table d'hôte was under the
veranda, and when he said it was, I took a seat at one of the tables and had an excellent
meal.

%%K t13-noto-2 noto

%%K t13-tit-2 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{An excellent lunch.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t13-09-1 p
9. --- The waiter brought me the lunch menu (*) and the wine list. I made my choice and
ordered \VUgras{prawns} with \VUgras{butter} as a
\VUgras{starter}, and, for the first course, \VUgras{tripe Caen style}. I had
no \VUgras{fish}, but asked for a portion of \VUgras{leg} of lamb and, as a
\VUgras{vegetable}, creamed \VUgras{spinach}. Then, since none of the
\VUgras{sweet courses} appealed to me, I had them bring a variety of desserts:
\VUgras{cheese}, \VUgras{fruit} and \VUgras{biscuits}. I added an excellent
Saint-Émilion \VUgras{wine}, and, very pleased with my meal, I left the table after giving the
\VUgras{waiter} \textsuperscript{(57)} a handsome \VUgras{tip} \textsuperscript{(59)}.

%%K t13-10-1 p
10. --- Seeing that I was ready to leave, the \VUgras{manager} \textsuperscript{(60)}
suggested I step out on to the balcony to admire the splendid panorama of the \VUgras{ocean}
\textsuperscript{(62)}. In the distance you could make out small fishing \VUgras{boats}
\textsuperscript{(63)}, \VUgras{sailing ships} \textsuperscript{(64)} and an enormous
\VUgras{liner} \textsuperscript{(65)} whose black silhouette stood out against the white
\VUgras{clouds} \textsuperscript{(67)} on the \VUgras{horizon} \textsuperscript{(66)}. A light
breeze stirred the \VUgras{flag} \textsuperscript{(70)} planted above the
\VUgras{sign} \textsuperscript{(69)} of the \VUgras{hall} \textsuperscript{(68)}.

%%K t13-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Amusements.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t13-11-1 p
11. --- The sight was so interesting that I decided to go up on to the café terrace the next
day, taking with me a pair of \VUgras{opera glasses} \textsuperscript{(71)} or a
\VUgras{telescope} \textsuperscript{(72)}.

%%K t13-12-1 p
12. --- On leaving the balcony I went to the hotel café for a
\VUgras{drink} \textsuperscript{(73)} and a game of \VUgras{billiards} \textsuperscript{(75)}. I
walked straight through the café room, where many customers were playing
\VUgras{chess} \textsuperscript{(78)}, \VUgras{draughts} \textsuperscript{(79)},
\VUgras{dominoes} \textsuperscript{(80)}, \VUgras{backgammon} \textsuperscript{(81)} or
\VUgras{cards} \textsuperscript{(82)}, and went straight up to the
\VUgras{billiard room} \textsuperscript{(74)}.

%%K t13-13-1 p
13. --- When the game was over, I went down to the ground floor and asked the waiter for an
\VUgras{envelope} \textsuperscript{(84)}, some \VUgras{paper} \textsuperscript{(83)}, a
\VUgras{postage stamp} \textsuperscript{(85)}, a \VUgras{pen} \textsuperscript{(87)} and some
\VUgras{ink} \textsuperscript{(86)} to write a letter.

%%K t13-13-2 p
Then I called a \VUgras{coachman} \textsuperscript{(92)} whose \VUgras{carriage}
\textsuperscript{(88)} had stopped in front of the café. I asked him his price by the hour and
by the trip, and when we had agreed, he opened the
\VUgras{carriage door} \textsuperscript{(89)} for me and settled me inside. He climbed
back on to his \VUgras{seat} \textsuperscript{(91)}, started the horses briskly with his
\VUgras{whip} \textsuperscript{(93)}, and off we went for a delightful drive.
