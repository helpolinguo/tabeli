%%K t03-c1-01-1 p
1. --- We are in \VUgras{spring}, in the month of \VUgras{March},
\VUgras{April} or \VUgras{May}, on a day of the \VUgras{week} ---
\VUgras{Monday}, \VUgras{Tuesday} or \VUgras{Wednesday}. It is
\VUgras{ten o'clock} in the morning.

%%K t03-c1-02-1 p
2. --- The \VUgras{weather} is fine, the \VUgras{air} clear. The sun is
shining. A few small \VUgras{clouds} \textsuperscript{(2)} are rising in the blue
\VUgras{sky} \textsuperscript{(1)}.

%%K t03-c1-03-1 p
3. --- The \VUgras{trees} have hardly any \VUgras{leaves}. The slender
\VUgras{poplars} \textsuperscript{(3)} and the tall \VUgras{plane tree}
\textsuperscript{(17)} still bear nothing but buds.

%%K t03-c1-04-1 p
4. --- The \VUgras{swallows} \textsuperscript{(4)} are back, and they wheel with
joyful cries around the \VUgras{spire} \textsuperscript{(7)} of the
\VUgras{bell tower} \textsuperscript{(5)} that crowns the village \VUgras{church}
\textsuperscript{(6)}.

%%K t03-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{3.4mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Church.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t03-c1-05-1 p
5. --- Very plain is this church with its great \VUgras{doorway}
\textsuperscript{(13)} and its big \VUgras{clock} \textsuperscript{(8)}. The small
\VUgras{hand} \textsuperscript{(9)} is on the figure X: it marks the
\VUgras{hours}. The \VUgras{big one} \textsuperscript{(10)} is on XII (noon); it
marks the \VUgras{minutes}.

%%K t03-c1-06-1 p
6. --- In the tower the \VUgras{bells} \textsuperscript{(11)} are ringing gaily.
At the top of the roof stands a small stone \VUgras{cross} \textsuperscript{(12)}.

%%K t03-c1-07-1 p
7. --- The church has not only a great \VUgras{doorway} \textsuperscript{(13)}, it
also has a small side door. Through this door the \VUgras{parish priest}
\textsuperscript{(15)}, in his \VUgras{cassock}, comes out of the \VUgras{vestry}
\textsuperscript{(14)} and makes his way to the \VUgras{presbytery} \textsuperscript{(19)}.

%%K t03-c1-08-1 p
8. --- Near him, here is the \VUgras{milkwoman} \textsuperscript{(24)} with her
\VUgras{donkey} \textsuperscript{(23)}. She is coming back from town, where she has
taken her good milk for the children.

%%K t03-tit-5 sub
\VUsaut{4.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The orchard.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t03-c1-09-1 p
9. --- Master Aliboron (the donkey) is quite pleased with this morning
run. He breathes in with delight the air scented by the \VUgras{blossom}
\textsuperscript{(20)} that covers the \VUgras{fruit trees} \textsuperscript{(18)} of the
neighbouring \VUgras{orchard}. All pink and white are those
\VUgras{peach trees} \textsuperscript{(18)} and \VUgras{apricot trees} \textsuperscript{(19)},
and they promise a fine crop.

%%K t03-c1-10-1 p
10. --- Meanwhile the little \VUgras{bees} \textsuperscript{(22)} from the
\VUgras{hive} \textsuperscript{(21)} go there to gather their sweet \VUgras{honey},
humming as they work.

%%K t03-c1-11-1 p
11. --- But what is happening? Here come the village children, running up
and scattering the frightened \VUgras{geese} \textsuperscript{(25)}. It is the
procession leaving the church after the \VUgras{christening} of the
notary's son.

%%K t03-c1-12-1 p
12. --- Little James, in his \VUgras{cradle} \textsuperscript{(26)}, wakes at the
joyful pealing of the bells, and his sister Madeleine, who wants to see
the procession too, asks her mother to come and comb her hair and dress
her on the doorstep.

%%K t03-c1-13-1 p
13. --- Her mother agrees. She puts on her \VUgras{headscarf}
\textsuperscript{(28)}, her \VUgras{bodice} \textsuperscript{(29)} and her \VUgras{apron}
\textsuperscript{(30)}, and comes and sits on a little chair in front of the door.
Madeleine has only her \VUgras{petticoat} \textsuperscript{(31)} and her
\VUgras{bodice} \textsuperscript{(32)}.

%%K t03-c1-14-1 p
14. --- How happy she is! She forgets her favourite flowers, her
\VUgras{carnations} \textsuperscript{(34)}, where the \VUgras{butterflies}
\textsuperscript{(35)} settle, her \VUgras{tulips} \textsuperscript{(36)}, her
\VUgras{lilies of the valley} \textsuperscript{(37)} in their \VUgras{pots}
\textsuperscript{(38)} on the \VUgras{wall} \textsuperscript{(33)}, and her \VUgras{roses}
\textsuperscript{(39)} that make so fine a hedge. She is gazing at the rich
clothes of the ladies in the procession.

%%K t03-c1-15-1 p
15. --- The village boys pay little attention to the clothes. They rush
forward and jostle one another to get \VUgras{sweets} \textsuperscript{(55)} or
\VUgras{coins} \textsuperscript{(52)}. One of them is crying \textsuperscript{(40)}.

%%K t03-c1-16-1 p
16. --- Another has trodden on the paw of the \VUgras{dog} \textsuperscript{(42)},
which runs off yelping and puts to flight the \VUgras{hen} \textsuperscript{(43)} and
her \VUgras{chicks} \textsuperscript{(44)}.

%%K t03-tit-6 sub
\VUsaut{5.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The christening procession.}
\VUsaut{4.0mm}

%%K t03-c1-17-1 p
17. --- At the head of the procession walks the \VUgras{nurse}
\textsuperscript{(45)}. She has a fine \VUgras{bonnet} \textsuperscript{(46)} with long
ribbons. She is carrying the \VUgras{newborn baby} \textsuperscript{(47)}, all
dressed in \VUgras{lace} \textsuperscript{(48)}.

%%K t03-c1-18-1 p
18. --- Behind the nurse come the \VUgras{godfather} \textsuperscript{(49)} and the
\VUgras{godmother} \textsuperscript{(53)}. They are handing out the sweets and the
coins. The godfather has \VUgras{gloves} \textsuperscript{(51)} and a \VUgras{top hat}
\textsuperscript{(50)}. The godmother is holding a fine \VUgras{bouquet}
\textsuperscript{(54)} in her hand.

%%K t03-c1-19-1 p
19. --- Behind them we can see the child's \VUgras{uncle} \textsuperscript{(56)} and
\VUgras{aunt} \textsuperscript{(58)}. The aunt has an elegant \VUgras{hat}
\textsuperscript{(59)}, the uncle a black \VUgras{frock coat} \textsuperscript{(57)} and a
white waistcoat. Next come the \VUgras{cousin} \textsuperscript{(60)} and his
\VUgras{cousin} \textsuperscript{(61)}. The latter is holding by the hand the
newborn baby's \VUgras{sister} \textsuperscript{(62)}, little Bertha.

%%K t03-c1-20-1 p
20. --- Bertha's other \VUgras{brother} \textsuperscript{(63)} walks behind. He is
giving his hand to a \VUgras{relative} \textsuperscript{(64)}. The family's
\VUgras{friends} \textsuperscript{(65)} come after them.

%%K t03-tit-7 sub
\VUsaut{4.6mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The onlookers.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t03-c1-21-1 p
21. --- Near the procession, here is a \VUgras{beggar} \textsuperscript{(66)} leaning
on his \VUgras{crutch} \textsuperscript{(67)}. He is asking for alms.

%%K t03-c1-22-1 p
22. --- On the other side is a very \VUgras{polite} \textsuperscript{(68)} little
boy. He raises his cap and wishes \VUgras{“good morning”} to the people he
knows.

%%K t03-c1-23-1 p
23. --- The villagers have come out of their houses to watch the
christening procession go by. We can see a \VUgras{countryman}
\textsuperscript{(69)} with his \VUgras{cotton cap} and beside him a
\VUgras{countrywoman} \textsuperscript{(70)}. Then an \VUgras{old woman}
\textsuperscript{(71)} leaning on her \VUgras{stick} \textsuperscript{(72)}. Lastly the
\VUgras{miller} \textsuperscript{(73)} with his broad \VUgras{felt hat} \textsuperscript{(74)},
and a young countrywoman in \VUgras{clogs} \textsuperscript{(75)} teaching her baby
to walk.

%%K t03-c1-24-1 p
24. --- It is a very lively scene.
\VUsaut{4.0mm}

%%K t03-tit-8 sub
\VUcentre{12.0pt}{0}{\textit{Second scene.}}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t03-tit-9 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{A public holiday.}
\VUsaut{2.4mm}

%%K t03-tit-10 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Water sports and regatta.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t03-c2-01-1 p
1. --- \VUgras{Summer} has come. The blazing sun of the month of
\VUgras{June} (July or \VUgras{August}) tempts the young people to bathe
and to go boating.

%%K t03-c2-02-1 p
2. --- On the right bank of the river, the oarsmen are undressing to take
part in the water sports and the regatta.

%%K t03-c2-03-1 p
3. --- One of them is taking off his \VUgras{shoes} \textsuperscript{(1)}; he is
unlacing (unbuttoning) them. His two friends are ready already. They now
have nothing on but their \VUgras{jersey} \textsuperscript{(2)} and
\VUgras{bathing trunks} \textsuperscript{(3)}. They are chatting as they wait for
their friends. They are talking about the fair and the festival being
held on the other bank.

%%K t03-c2-04-1 p
4. --- On the ground near them lie their friends' \VUgras{clothes}: a
\VUgras{pair} of \VUgras{boots} \textsuperscript{(4)}, \VUgras{shoes} \textsuperscript{(5)},
\VUgras{socks} \textsuperscript{(6)}, \VUgras{felt} \textsuperscript{(7)} and \VUgras{straw}
\textsuperscript{(8)} hats and a \VUgras{tie} \textsuperscript{(9)}.

%%K t03-c2-05-1 p
5. --- One of the young men has carefully folded his \VUgras{jacket}
\textsuperscript{(10)}. He is laying it on a towel, in which his neighbour will
shortly wrap up both their \VUgras{suits}.

%%K t03-c2-06-1 p
6. --- A sixth boy is behindhand. He still has his \VUgras{cap}
\textsuperscript{(11)} on his head. He has taken off neither his \VUgras{shirt}
\textsuperscript{(12)}, nor his \VUgras{collar} \textsuperscript{(13)}, nor his
\VUgras{cuffs} \textsuperscript{(14)}. He is holding his \VUgras{waistcoat}
\textsuperscript{(17)} in his hand and still has his \VUgras{trousers} \textsuperscript{(16)}
and \VUgras{braces} \textsuperscript{(15)} on. He will certainly not be ready for
the next race.

%%K t03-c2-07-1 p
7. --- Near the young men, a path lined with \VUgras{thistles}
\textsuperscript{(18)} leads down to the riverside, where old James is getting
ready, among the \VUgras{reeds} \textsuperscript{(19)}, the \VUgras{fireworks}
\textsuperscript{(20)} that will be set off in the evening.

%%K t03-tit-11 sub
\VUsaut{4.4mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The race.}
\VUsaut{3.4mm}

%%K t03-c2-08-1 p
8. --- On the river, a few ladies sheltering under their parasols are out
in a \VUgras{rowing boat} \textsuperscript{(21)}. They are following the race, which
is very exciting. In each \VUgras{skiff} \textsuperscript{(22)}, four \VUgras{rowers}
\textsuperscript{(24)} are pulling with all their strength, while the
\VUgras{coxswain} \textsuperscript{(26)} holds the \VUgras{rudder} \textsuperscript{(25)} and
steers the fragile craft. The \VUgras{oars} \textsuperscript{(23)} strike the water
in time, and the light boats race along at a dizzying speed.

%%K t03-c2-09-1 p
9. --- A few young men are competing, near the pier of the bridge, for
the prize of the \VUgras{greasy pole} \textsuperscript{(28)}. One of them is edging
carefully along the springy, slippery pole to reach the little
\VUgras{flag} \textsuperscript{(29)} fixed at the end. His luckless
\VUgras{rival} \textsuperscript{(30)} has fallen into the water. He is swimming back
to shore.

%%K t03-c2-10-1 p
10. --- Older lads are \VUgras{water-jousting} \textsuperscript{(32)} near the
\VUgras{quay} \textsuperscript{(33)}. A large \VUgras{crowd} \textsuperscript{(31)} is
watching all these games, leaning on the \VUgras{parapet} of the
\VUgras{bridge} \textsuperscript{(27)} and packed along the \VUgras{bank}. A few
onlookers, though, are turning round to follow the game of the
\VUgras{climbing pole} \textsuperscript{(45)}, or to admire the
\VUgras{mayoral procession} on its way to the \VUgras{prize-giving}.

%%K t03-c2-11-1 p
11. --- First, here are a few \VUgras{urchins} \textsuperscript{(35)} running ahead
of the \VUgras{bandsmen} \textsuperscript{(36)}; then come the \VUgras{mayor}
\textsuperscript{(37)}, his deputies and the \VUgras{town council} of the little
town. Last of all march the \VUgras{firemen} \textsuperscript{(38)}.

%%K t03-tit-12 sub
\VUsaut{5.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The fair.}
\VUsaut{3.6mm}

%%K t03-c2-12-1 p
12. --- There is a great crowd on the \VUgras{fairground} \textsuperscript{(39)}.
People come to see the various \VUgras{booths}: the \VUgras{wooden horses}
\textsuperscript{(40)}, the \VUgras{circus} \textsuperscript{(41)}, where the performance has
already begun; the \VUgras{public dance} \textsuperscript{(42)}, where the young men
and women of the town are enjoying the pleasure of \VUgras{dancing}.

%%K t03-c2-13-1 p
13. --- At the far end we can see the \VUgras{arena} \textsuperscript{(44)}, where
the brave Spanish \VUgras{matador} fights the furious \VUgras{bull}
\textsuperscript{(45)}; then the \VUgras{switchback} \textsuperscript{(49)} and the
\VUgras{shooting gallery} \textsuperscript{(46)}, where people practise handling
\VUgras{firearms} and shooting at the \VUgras{target}.

%%K t03-c2-14-1 p
14. --- Close by, here is the \VUgras{fairground theatre} \textsuperscript{(47)},
where \VUgras{comedy} and \VUgras{drama} are performed. Very near it stands
the booth of the \VUgras{giant} \textsuperscript{(48)} and the \VUgras{dwarf}. The
\VUgras{height} of the first is two metres fifteen; the second is barely as
tall as a child.

%%K t03-c2-15-1 p
15. --- Lastly, here is the great \VUgras{menagerie} \textsuperscript{(50)} with its
\VUgras{wild animals}, its \VUgras{tiger} \textsuperscript{(51)} with its powerful
\VUgras{claws}, its \VUgras{lion} \textsuperscript{(52)} with its thick \VUgras{mane},
its two \VUgras{giraffes} \textsuperscript{(53)} and its \VUgras{elephant}
\textsuperscript{(54)}, which uses its \VUgras{trunk} so cleverly.

%%K t03-c2-16-1 p
16. --- The \VUgras{tamer} \textsuperscript{(55)} who trains these animals is
standing at the door of the booth.
