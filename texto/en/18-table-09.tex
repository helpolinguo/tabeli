%%K t09-c1-01-1 p
1. --- I have been at Pratz for a week. I cannot express all the wonder I
felt when I stood before the marvellous \VUgras{mountain range}
\textsuperscript{(1)} of the Pyrenees, whose \VUgras{crest} \textsuperscript{(2)} stands out
against the blue sky like a giant scallop.

%%K t09-c1-02-1 p
2. --- I had not imagined that the Pyrenean \VUgras{summits} \textsuperscript{(3)}
could reach so great a \VUgras{height}, and I was left astonished at the
magnificent \VUgras{glaciers} \textsuperscript{(5)} and the snow-covered
\VUgras{peaks} \textsuperscript{(4)} down which such terrifying \VUgras{avalanches}
sweep.

%%K t09-tit-3 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Mountain landscape.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c1-03-1 p
3. --- The very day after I arrived, I went to the old extinct
\VUgras{volcano} \textsuperscript{(6)} near Pratz. On the bare, barren rim of the
\VUgras{crater} one can still clearly see the traces left by the
\VUgras{lava} that flowed, several centuries ago, down the
\VUgras{mountainside} \textsuperscript{(7)}. On one of the \VUgras{slopes} I managed
to find a few fragments of that lava.

%%K t09-c1-04-1 p
4. --- Pratz stands on the frontier, and the \VUgras{fortress}
\textsuperscript{(8)} that guards the \VUgras{pass} \textsuperscript{(27)} between France
and Spain is quite like those old castles on the banks of the Rhine that
seem to be watching for \VUgras{prey} from the top of their rock.

%%K t09-c1-05-1 p
5. --- A huge \VUgras{eagle} \textsuperscript{(9)}, which has its eyrie not far from
the \VUgras{fort} \textsuperscript{(8)}, often catches my attention. This
\VUgras{bird of prey}, with its powerful \VUgras{beak}, often brings its
young a lamb or a kid held in its \VUgras{talons}. Its \VUgras{wings} are so
large that it can glide for a long while with its heavy load.

%%K t09-tit-4 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Walker.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c1-06-1 p
6. --- The pleasantest walk here is the trip to the \VUgras{waterfall}
\textsuperscript{(10)} of Cau. The \VUgras{falling water} tumbles down in a swirl and
then disappears as a pretty \VUgras{stream} in the \VUgras{fir wood}
\textsuperscript{(11)} west of the town. We climbed through this wood as far as
the \VUgras{weather station} \textsuperscript{(12)}, and from the top of the
\VUgras{lookout} we took in the whole \VUgras{panorama} of the district. The
\VUgras{landscape} is superb, and \VUgras{nature} seems to have lavished on
this country every treasure at her disposal.

%%K t09-c1-07-1 p
7. --- To come down again we took the \VUgras{funicular} \textsuperscript{(13)} and
stopped at a small \VUgras{station} near the \VUgras{ruins} \textsuperscript{(14)} of
an old \VUgras{feudal castle} once lived in by the lords of Pratz.

%%K t09-c1-08-1 p
8. --- Poor castle! What must it think when it sees below it the
neat \VUgras{spa town} \textsuperscript{(15)}, now a fashionable
\VUgras{thermal resort}?

%%K t09-c1-08-2 p
The \VUgras{climate} is so pleasant and the \VUgras{mineral water} so
beneficial that the \VUgras{course of treatment} taken at the
\VUgras{sanatorium} \textsuperscript{(17)} is a real pleasure.

%%K t09-tit-5 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{A pleasant spa town.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c1-09-1 p
9. --- Everything, indeed, has been done to make the stay agreeable. A
great \VUgras{viaduct} \textsuperscript{(16)} was built to carry a railway; the
\VUgras{park} \textsuperscript{(18)} of the \VUgras{pump room}, where
\VUgras{concerts} are given, is laid out charmingly. Several graceful
\VUgras{villas} \textsuperscript{(19)} have been built around the \VUgras{casino}
\textsuperscript{(21)}, and a very well kept \VUgras{highway} \textsuperscript{(22)} makes
getting about extremely easy.

%%K t09-c1-10-1 p
10. --- A simple \VUgras{embankment} \textsuperscript{(23)} separates the
\VUgras{road} \textsuperscript{(20)} from the \VUgras{valley} \textsuperscript{(25)} proper,
at the bottom of which lies a graceful little \VUgras{lake} \textsuperscript{(26)}
that feeds a clear \VUgras{stream} \textsuperscript{(24)}.

%%K t09-c1-11-1 p
11. --- On the other side of the valley an iron \VUgras{mine} \textsuperscript{(28)}
is being worked. Several times I went to watch the \VUgras{miners} going
down the \VUgras{shaft}, and I even went into the \VUgras{gallery} where they
spend their day extracting the \VUgras{ore} that holds the \VUgras{metal}.

%%K t09-c1-12-1 p
12. --- A large \VUgras{foundry} was built beside the mine to use at once
the riches taken out of it. The \VUgras{blast furnace} \textsuperscript{(29)},
heated with the \VUgras{coal} that is plentiful here, makes it possible to
obtain \VUgras{pig iron} quickly and cheaply.

%%K t09-c1-13-1 p
13. --- Not far from the mine a \VUgras{quarry} \textsuperscript{(30)} is being cut.
It is neither \VUgras{granite}, nor \VUgras{porphyry}, nor even
\VUgras{sandstone} that the old \VUgras{quarryman} breaks off with his
\VUgras{pick}, but plain \VUgras{building stone} for putting up houses.

%%K t09-c1-14-1 p
14. --- One of the finest ornaments of this country is the \VUgras{fir tree}
\textsuperscript{(31)}. Everywhere --- at the bottom of a \VUgras{ravine}
\textsuperscript{(32)}, on the bank of a \VUgras{torrent} \textsuperscript{(33)}, beside a
\VUgras{chasm} \textsuperscript{(34)} or a precipice --- its slender needles add
their note of green.

%%K t09-tit-6 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Walking.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c1-15-1 p
15. --- I am making the most of my holidays to take long walks. The
\VUgras{paths} \textsuperscript{(35)} that wind up the mountain make it possible to
cover several \VUgras{kilometres}, and often several \VUgras{leagues},
without noticing the distance.

%%K t09-c1-15-2 p
\VUgras{Milestones} \textsuperscript{(36)} and \VUgras{signposts} \textsuperscript{(37)} mark
out the paths, on which one often meets a young \VUgras{mountain lad}
\textsuperscript{(38)} with a \VUgras{knapsack} \textsuperscript{(40)} at his side, carrying
a \VUgras{marmot} \textsuperscript{(39)}, or some \VUgras{gypsy} \textsuperscript{(41)} whose
\VUgras{fur cap} \textsuperscript{(42)} contrasts oddly with his torn old
\VUgras{hood} \textsuperscript{(43)}. He leads a performing \VUgras{bear}
\textsuperscript{(44)} on a \VUgras{chain}.

%%K t09-tit-7 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Climbing.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c1-16-1 p
16. --- Almost every day I go with a \VUgras{guide} \textsuperscript{(48)} to make a
\VUgras{climb}, and I am very proud when, with my \VUgras{pack}
\textsuperscript{(47)} on my back and my \VUgras{alpenstock} in my hand, like a real
\VUgras{tourist} \textsuperscript{(46)}, I attack the \VUgras{rocks} \textsuperscript{(45)}.

%%K t09-c1-17-1 p
17. --- From the top of a mountain, the people of the plain look no bigger
than ants; a \VUgras{flock of sheep} \textsuperscript{(50)} grazing in a
\VUgras{pasture} \textsuperscript{(49)} looks like a child's toy. A \VUgras{pine}
\textsuperscript{(51)}, or even a \VUgras{wooden chalet} \textsuperscript{(52)}, is hardly
more than a speck on the green.

%%K t09-c1-18-1 p
18. --- On these outings we often meet on the \VUgras{track} \textsuperscript{(53)} a
\VUgras{chamois hunter} \textsuperscript{(54)} carrying on his shoulder the animal he
has just killed.

%%K t09-c1-19-1 p
19. --- I have put in my herbarium a very remarkable frond of \VUgras{fern}
\textsuperscript{(55)} and a pretty pink sprig of \VUgras{heather} \textsuperscript{(57)},
which I gathered at the mouth of a little \VUgras{cave} \textsuperscript{(56)} on my
last walk.

%%K t09-tit-8 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c2-01-1 p
1. --- Yesterday I spent a delightful day in the forest. The busy life of
the animals and the \VUgras{insects} \textsuperscript{(5)} that live there interested
me greatly.

%%K t09-c2-01-2 p
The \VUgras{spider} \textsuperscript{(1)} spinning its \VUgras{web} \textsuperscript{(2)}
between two branches to catch the passing \VUgras{fly} \textsuperscript{(3)}, the
\VUgras{snail} \textsuperscript{(4)} labouring along with its shell, the stag beetle
scouting for its prey, the busy ant hard at work near the \VUgras{anthill}
\textsuperscript{(6)} --- all these small creatures came and went and worked with
extraordinary energy.

%%K t09-c2-02-1 p
2. --- A \VUgras{snake} \textsuperscript{(7)}, which at first I took for a
\VUgras{viper} but which was only a harmless \VUgras{grass snake}, was
curled up under a tree trunk, now and then poking out its slender little
head, which always seemed ready to bite. In front of me a big \VUgras{slug}
\textsuperscript{(8)} was crawling heavily along after devouring one of the tasty
little \VUgras{wild strawberries} \textsuperscript{(11)} that grow here in
abundance.

%%K t09-c2-03-1 p
3. --- The ground was carpeted with \VUgras{woodland flowers}:
\VUgras{primroses} \textsuperscript{(9)}, \VUgras{periwinkles} \textsuperscript{(10)} and
many other plants I did not know were in bloom among the
\VUgras{tufts of grass} \textsuperscript{(12)}.

%%K t09-c2-04-1 p
4. --- In this district the \VUgras{truffle} is almost as common as the
\VUgras{mushroom} \textsuperscript{(14)}, and a \VUgras{young pig} \textsuperscript{(13)}
belonging to a forester was greedily hunting to see whether it could find
some.

%%K t09-tit-10 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Poaching.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c2-05-1 p
5. --- I saw the \VUgras{end} of a scene that amused me greatly. Thanks to
the nose of his \VUgras{basset hound} \textsuperscript{(15)}, the \VUgras{forester}
\textsuperscript{(16)} had just surprised a \VUgras{poacher} \textsuperscript{(22)} at the
moment when the man was getting ready to carry off the \VUgras{game}
\textsuperscript{(17)} he had killed. Preferring to give up his catch rather than
be arrested, the poacher had fled, and \VUgras{hare} \textsuperscript{(18)},
\VUgras{hind} \textsuperscript{(19)}, \VUgras{roe deer} \textsuperscript{(20)} and
\VUgras{wild boar} \textsuperscript{(21)} lay on the grass, to the forester's
delight.

%%K t09-c2-06-1 p
6. --- The variety of trees the forest holds is astonishing. The
\VUgras{beeches} \textsuperscript{(23)} and the \VUgras{birches} \textsuperscript{(26)}, the
\VUgras{pines}, which yield \VUgras{resin} \textsuperscript{(27)}, the \VUgras{oaks}
\textsuperscript{(29)} and many others besides mingle their branches.

%%K t09-c2-06-2 p
The \VUgras{ivy} \textsuperscript{(24)} clinging to the trunks of the tall trees
gives the \VUgras{high wood} \textsuperscript{(25)} a brilliant green, which blends
pleasantly with the paler, softer shade of the \VUgras{moss} \textsuperscript{(31)}
in which the \VUgras{green woodpecker} \textsuperscript{(30)} hunts for insects.

%%K t09-tit-11 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Forest landscape.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c2-07-1 p
7. --- The old oaks charmed me. Their big twisted \VUgras{roots}
\textsuperscript{(32)}, half out of the ground, give them a splendid look of
strength and vigour, and I wonder how the \VUgras{owner} \textsuperscript{(35)} can
bring himself to have them felled and handed over to the
\VUgras{saw} \textsuperscript{(34)} of the \VUgras{woodcutter} \textsuperscript{(33)}. The
poor \VUgras{trunks} \textsuperscript{(36)}, stripped of their \VUgras{bark}
\textsuperscript{(37)} or split by the \VUgras{axe} \textsuperscript{(38)} of the
\VUgras{day labourer} \textsuperscript{(39)}, grieve me.

%%K t09-c2-08-1 p
8. --- Close by, an old \VUgras{charcoal burner} \textsuperscript{(41)} had built up
with his \VUgras{shovel} a \VUgras{stack} \textsuperscript{(42)} of charcoal, and an
old woman was carrying away a \VUgras{bundle} \textsuperscript{(40)} of
\VUgras{dead wood} made of twigs from the felled trees.

%%K t09-tit-12 sub
\VUsaut{3.0mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Hunt.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t09-c2-09-1 p
9. --- I meant to go and rest for a while at the \VUgras{forester's house}
\textsuperscript{(46)}, but when I reached the little \VUgras{pond} \textsuperscript{(43)},
on which a handsome \VUgras{swan} \textsuperscript{(45)} was swimming, I noticed that
a recent \VUgras{flood} \textsuperscript{(44)} had turned the path into a real
\VUgras{marsh}. I had to make a detour, and as I passed the \VUgras{cedar}
\textsuperscript{(49)}, the \VUgras{poplars} \textsuperscript{(48)} and the \VUgras{elm}
\textsuperscript{(47)} that stand on the edge of the forest, I saw a magnificent
\VUgras{stag} \textsuperscript{(50)} break out of a \VUgras{thicket} \textsuperscript{(54)},
chased by a relentless \VUgras{pack of hounds} \textsuperscript{(51)} urged on by
the \VUgras{horn} \textsuperscript{(53)} of the \VUgras{mounted huntsmen}
\textsuperscript{(52)}. The poor beast was utterly spent. It fell dying a few paces
from me.

%%K t09-c2-10-1 p
10. --- The forester's son, drawn out by the noise of the \VUgras{hunt}, came
out of the house, and the two of us went back into the forest. He had
seen a \VUgras{magpie} \textsuperscript{(56)} on the branch of an old oak. He thought
its nest must be hidden in the \VUgras{foliage} \textsuperscript{(58)} and wanted to
take it.

%%K t09-c2-10-2 p
Nimble as a \VUgras{squirrel} \textsuperscript{(61)}, he climbed from \VUgras{branch}
\textsuperscript{(55)} to branch, but he found nothing and managed only to startle
an old \VUgras{crow} \textsuperscript{(60)} perched on a \VUgras{bough}
\textsuperscript{(57)}.
