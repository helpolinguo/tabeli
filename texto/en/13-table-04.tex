%%K t04-c1-01-1 p
1. --- The young people have grown up. One of them, John, is getting
married. We are at the \VUgras{wedding breakfast}, which brings the
families of the bride and groom together around the same table.

%%K t04-c1-02-1 p
2. --- We are in \VUgras{autumn}, in the month of \VUgras{September}. The
cold wind of \VUgras{October} and \VUgras{November} has not yet stripped the
countryside of its green leaves. The evening air is mild and fragrant; so
dinner is being served under the \VUgras{veranda} \textsuperscript{(8)} that opens on
to the garden.

%%K t04-c1-03-1 p
3. --- In the distance, on the \VUgras{hill} \textsuperscript{(3)}, stands the home
of John's parents, an elegant \VUgras{country house} \textsuperscript{(1)} hidden in
the greenery; fine vineyards, which yield an excellent wine, cover the
slope of the hillside. The winegrower tends and cares for them.

%%K t04-c1-04-1 p
4. --- Above the vines, a covey of \VUgras{partridges} \textsuperscript{(2)} goes by,
startled by the approach of the \VUgras{gamekeeper} \textsuperscript{(5)}. He, with
his \VUgras{gun} under his arm and his \VUgras{game bag slung over his
shoulder}, is beating the \VUgras{bushes} \textsuperscript{(4)} with his \VUgras{dog}
\textsuperscript{(6)}. He watches over the estate and keeps poachers from
destroying the game.

%%K t04-c1-05-1 p
5. --- Outside the \VUgras{veranda} climbs a \VUgras{trained vine}, from which
hang heavy \VUgras{bunches of grapes} \textsuperscript{(7)}.

%%K t04-c1-06-1 p
6. --- The fine autumn flowers that decorate the \VUgras{table} \textsuperscript{(16)}
are \VUgras{chrysanthemums} \textsuperscript{(9)}; the bride's \VUgras{father}
\textsuperscript{(10)} has put one in the buttonhole of his tailcoat.

%%K t04-c1-07-1 p
7. --- The meal is drawing to a close. The \VUgras{servant} \textsuperscript{(11)} is
beginning to bring in the dessert dishes and will soon clear away the
various parts of the place settings --- \VUgras{spoons} \textsuperscript{(14)},
\VUgras{forks} \textsuperscript{(12)}, \VUgras{knives} \textsuperscript{(13)},
\VUgras{serving dishes} \textsuperscript{(15)} --- that are no longer needed.

%%K t04-tit-3 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Family.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t04-c1-08-1 p
8. --- Everyone looks cheerful, and the smile with which the groom's
\VUgras{mother} \textsuperscript{(17)} watches her children shows how happy she is.

%%K t04-c1-09-1 p
9. --- This marriage unites two wealthy families of the district. John,
the \VUgras{husband} \textsuperscript{(18)}, is the \VUgras{son} of a great landowner,
and he becomes the \VUgras{son-in-law} of a successful manufacturer.

%%K t04-c1-10-1 p
10. --- The \VUgras{couple} are young, and yet they have been
\VUgras{engaged} for a long time. The \VUgras{young wife} \textsuperscript{(19)},
Martha, looks charming in her \VUgras{white dress} trimmed with orange
blossom.

%%K t04-c1-11-1 p
11. --- Her \VUgras{father-in-law} \textsuperscript{(20)} is delighted to have so
kind a \VUgras{daughter-in-law}, and John's \VUgras{mother-in-law}
\textsuperscript{(21)} smiles to see her \VUgras{daughter} looking so lovely.

%%K t04-c1-12-1 p
12. --- At the end of the table sit the other \VUgras{guests}
\textsuperscript{(22)}: \VUgras{relatives, friends, cousins}. A \VUgras{head waiter}
\textsuperscript{(23)}, with his napkin over his arm, is attending to them
briskly.

%%K t04-tit-4 sub
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Friends.}
\VUsaut{3.0mm}

%%K t04-c1-13-1 p
13. --- On the right-hand side of the table, here is a \VUgras{young lady}
\textsuperscript{(24)}, a \VUgras{friend} of the bride, then the bride's
\VUgras{brother}, who is her \VUgras{best man} \textsuperscript{(25)}. He is chatting
with his \VUgras{bridesmaid} \textsuperscript{(26)}, the groom's sister, who by this
marriage becomes Martha's \VUgras{sister-in-law}. She is a charming young
woman. She has beautiful \VUgras{jewellery}, a \VUgras{pearl necklace} and a
gold \VUgras{bracelet}.

%%K t04-c1-14-1 p
14. --- Near them, the gentleman standing and raising his glass is a
\VUgras{friend} \textsuperscript{(27)} of the family. He is one of the
\VUgras{groom's witnesses}. He is proposing a \VUgras{toast}, drinking to
the \VUgras{health} of the newly-weds and wishing them long happiness. He
is in formal dress: tailcoat and \VUgras{patent leather shoes}
\textsuperscript{(28)}. He is escorting the \VUgras{grandmother} \textsuperscript{(29)}, who
is a \VUgras{widow}.

%%K t04-c1-15-1 p
15. --- The young man in the \VUgras{tailcoat} \textsuperscript{(31)}, with the thin
black moustache, is John's \VUgras{brother-in-law} \textsuperscript{(30)}. He is a
distinguished engineer. He is sitting next to a very elegant
\VUgras{young lady} \textsuperscript{(33)}, Martha's \VUgras{elder sister} and the
mother of the sweet little girl who is coming, on her cousin's arm, to
offer a flower and \VUgras{wish} her \VUgras{good evening}. This lady has
very fine \VUgras{earrings} and an elegant \VUgras{hairstyle}.

%%K t04-c1-16-1 p
16. --- The little cousin, who looks so odd in his \VUgras{smock}
\textsuperscript{(36)} and his baggy \VUgras{breeches} \textsuperscript{(37)}, is much to be
pitied. He is an \VUgras{orphan} \textsuperscript{(35)}. He lost his father and his
mother when he was very young. His uncle is his \VUgras{guardian}
\textsuperscript{(38)}, and stayed single in order to bring the poor child up. He
is a kind man. He is rather bald and very short-sighted; he wears
\VUgras{pince-nez}.

%%K t04-tit-5 sub
\VUsaut{2.4mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{The Dessert.}

%%K t04-c1-17-1 p
17. --- Behind the guests, on a table covered with a \VUgras{tablecloth},
the \VUgras{dessert} \textsuperscript{(39)} is laid out. In a large bowl, here is
fruit: \VUgras{peaches} \textsuperscript{(40)}, \VUgras{pears} \textsuperscript{(41)},
\VUgras{apples} \textsuperscript{(42)}, \VUgras{apricots} \textsuperscript{(43)} and
\VUgras{grapes} \textsuperscript{(44)}. Beside them, \VUgras{pineapples}
\textsuperscript{(45)}, a fine \VUgras{cake} \textsuperscript{(46)},
\VUgras{bottles of liqueur} \textsuperscript{(48)} and \VUgras{champagne}
\textsuperscript{(49)}, and stacks of clean \VUgras{plates} \textsuperscript{(47)} as well.

%%K t04-c1-18-1 p
18. --- The \VUgras{wine waiter} \textsuperscript{(50)} is uncorking a \VUgras{bottle}
\textsuperscript{(52)} of old wine with a \VUgras{corkscrew} \textsuperscript{(51)}. The
\VUgras{cork} \textsuperscript{(53)} seems very hard to draw. This wine waiter has a
\VUgras{napkin} \textsuperscript{(54)} over his arm.

%%K t04-c1-19-1 p
19. --- After dinner the gentlemen will move on to the smoking room, and
the ladies will go and get ready for the ball.

%%K t04-c2-01-1 p
1. --- Ten years have passed. John's parents have grown old, and they
dearly love their three grandchildren, two \VUgras{grandsons}
\textsuperscript{(2)} and a \VUgras{granddaughter} \textsuperscript{(3)}. As today is
\VUgras{grandfather's birthday}, the children have come to wish him many
happy returns.

%%K t04-c2-02-1 p
2. --- Little Peter is still very young; he is only three. He sees the
\VUgras{cat} \textsuperscript{(1)} coming towards him. He wants to stroke its
beautiful silky \VUgras{fur} and its long \VUgras{tail}; it does not occur to
him that, under those graceful \VUgras{paws}, there are nasty sharp claws
that may well scratch him.

%%K t04-c2-03-1 p
3. --- His sister Gabrielle, who is holding his hand, is much more
serious, and Marcel, with his big \VUgras{overcoat} \textsuperscript{(4)} and leather
\VUgras{belt} \textsuperscript{(5)}, looks quite the little man, in spite of the
short breeches that leave his \VUgras{stockings} \textsuperscript{(6)} showing.

%%K t04-c2-04-1 p
4. --- Their father has put on his thick winter \VUgras{overcoat}
\textsuperscript{(7)} with its \VUgras{fur collar} \textsuperscript{(8)}, and in his
\VUgras{pocket} \textsuperscript{(9)} he has a scarf. His wife has her felt hat
trimmed with \VUgras{feathers} \textsuperscript{(10)}, her \VUgras{jacket}
\textsuperscript{(11)}, her fur \VUgras{boa} \textsuperscript{(12)} and her \VUgras{muff}
\textsuperscript{(13)}.

%%K t04-c2-05-1 p
5. --- It is very cold, for winter is here. The \VUgras{snow} \textsuperscript{(19)}
has been falling all day and the roofs of the houses are quite white.
With \VUgras{night} the cold grows sharper still. In the sky, the
\VUgras{moon} \textsuperscript{(17)} and the \VUgras{stars} \textsuperscript{(18)} shine and
pierce the \VUgras{darkness}.

\VUsaut{4.2mm}
\VUpk{10.2pt}{40}{Illness.}

%%K t04-c2-06-1 p
6. --- The poor \VUgras{blind man} \textsuperscript{(20)} crossing the square in
front of the \VUgras{chemist's shop} \textsuperscript{(16)}, led by his
\VUgras{poodle} \textsuperscript{(21)}, is very unfortunate. Justine, the
\VUgras{maid} \textsuperscript{(14)}, is bringing from the kitchen a \VUgras{bowl}
\textsuperscript{(15)} of very hot \VUgras{herbal tea}, generously sweetened.

%%K t04-c2-07-1 p
7. --- \VUgras{Grandmother} \textsuperscript{(23)}, who wants to fetch her
\VUgras{spectacles} \textsuperscript{(26)} from the table so as to read the
\VUgras{prescription} \textsuperscript{(27)} the \VUgras{doctor} \textsuperscript{(25)} has

\VUgras{stick} \textsuperscript{(24)}.

%%K t04-c2-08-1 p
8. --- She often suffers from small \VUgras{ailments}, \VUgras{dizzy spells},
\VUgras{colds} and \VUgras{toothache}; her legs are weak and her eyes are no

\VUgras{doctor}, who always wants to prescribe her a \VUgras{draught}

%%K t04-c2-09-1 p
9. --- It is snug in the well-shut room. In the marble \VUgras{fireplace}
\textsuperscript{(29)} a \VUgras{splendid fire} \textsuperscript{(30)} is blazing, and one

\VUsaut{4.2mm}

%%K t04-c2-10-1 p
10. --- Even \VUgras{grandfather} \textsuperscript{(33)} has forgotten that he has
been ill, that his \VUgras{rheumatism} keeps him fixed in his
\VUgras{armchair} \textsuperscript{(34)}, and that only yesterday he had a
\VUgras{fever and fainting fits}. He no longer remembers that last winter

suffer a great deal.

%%K t04-c2-10-2 p

his leg, which he rests on a \VUgras{cushion} \textsuperscript{(38)} laid on a small
\VUgras{stool} \textsuperscript{(39)}, still hurts him too.

%%K t04-c2-11-1 p
11. --- When he is carefully wrapped in his warm \VUgras{dressing gown}
\textsuperscript{(35)} with its big mother-of-pearl \VUgras{buttons} \textsuperscript{(36)},

\VUgras{orphan girl} \textsuperscript{(40)} in her white \VUgras{bonnet}, her blue
\VUgras{dress} \textsuperscript{(42)} and \VUgras{cape} \textsuperscript{(41)}, he does not
complain.

%%K t04-c2-12-1 p
12. --- Every year, when the \VUgras{calendar} \textsuperscript{(43)} brings round
the \VUgras{date} of the first of \VUgras{December} again, he waits
impatiently for his \VUgras{grandchildren}, for he knows they will not
forget that important \VUgras{date}.

%%K t04-c2-13-1 p
13. --- He remembers with feeling the far-off days when he used to go
himself to wish many happy returns to the glorious imperial
\VUgras{marshal}, whose \VUgras{portrait} \textsuperscript{(44)} hangs on the wall, and
who is the children's \VUgras{great-grandfather}.
